Overview

One place that controls who logs into every router, switch and firewall, and records every command they run.

Large networks are administered by many engineers and contractors. Alepo TACACS+ gives each of them their own login, decides on each command and records what was done.

ForNetwork and security teams with large device estates

Cisco ACS replacement

Cisco Secure ACS has reached end of life. Alepo TACACS+ is a supported replacement, and it also replaces local device passwords.

TACACS+ Server

Audit

A tamper-evident trail records every login and command and is forwarded to Splunk, Sentinel or QRadar. A control map to NIST SP 800-53 is included for auditors.

TACACS+ Server

Contractor access

Removing a contractor is one change in one place. No device has to be touched.

TACACS+ Server

AI network operations

Every Alepo product comes with its own agent. It watches that product's live data around the clock and brings a person the finding, with a proposed next step.

Nothing changes until that person approves.
