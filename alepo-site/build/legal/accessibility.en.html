Standard

Alepo aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on this website.

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and the focus is always visible.
  • Text and controls meet the AA contrast ratios.
  • Images have text alternatives.
  • Motion stops for visitors whose device asks for reduced motion.
  • Headings, landmarks and labels are in place for screen readers.

How it was checked

Every page was checked with automated tests in November 2026. The menus, tabs, questions and language switch were also checked with a keyboard. Automated tests do not find every barrier, so tell us if you meet one.

Known limits

Blog posts, news items and library pages come from the previous website and may not meet the same standard yet.

Feedback

If something on this website does not work for you, tell us through the contact page.
